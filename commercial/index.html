Food Service Comfort

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

Few buildings work an HVAC system harder than a restaurant. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods draw conditioned air out all shift long, and the makeup air unit has to replace that air without upsetting the building's pressure. Sparks HVAC services, sizes, and repairs the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we schedule service around your operating hours.

Staff can't work a hot line in a kitchen that's pulling negative pressure, and guests notice the drafts at the door and the heat right away. We keep temperatures and airflow where your operation needs them, then leave behind written reports and service logs after each visit. Those records show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and cover the documentation many commercial leases call for.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits the hours you're open. If a job can't be done during service, we install during off-hours, keeping the kitchen running and the dining room comfortable throughout.

Safety

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm correct venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. You receive a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Rapid Response

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

Each hour without climate control costs a business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. In a retail store, it empties the aisles on a summer afternoon. For a server room, it risks overheated equipment, and in a leased building it leads to tenant disputes. Sparks HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Sparks and surrounding areas, dispatching an experienced technician quickly when you call.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. It might be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, a faulty ignition system or gas valve, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board lockout. Whatever it is, we diagnose it fast, restore heating or cooling, and record every step for your service records. Then we put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.
